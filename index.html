The need in our neighborhoods

Nearly three in four young children within about 20 minutes of our campus live in homes where every parent works. In the ZIP codes closest to us, we estimate more children need care than there are licensed seats.

What happens before kindergarten matters. Only 44% of Maryland kindergartners arrive ready for school. Children who attended a formal early learning program are twice as likely to be ready as children in home or informal care.

Sources: Maryland State Department of Education, Readiness Matters Report 2023-24 (the figures show a link, not proof of cause); Greater Baltimore Church analysis of U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2019-2023 and state child care licensing data (estimates). What children will experience

Ready for kindergarten
A pre-K readiness curriculum aligned to Maryland's early learning standards.
Faith and character
Faith-based education that builds character as well as skill.
Care through the year
Summer enrichment for school-age children, opening {{ s.camp_opening }}.
A nurse on site
A full-time school nurse will serve the children.
Tuition families can manage
Tuition will be set within the range of Maryland's Child Care Scholarship, so working families do not have to choose between cost and quality.

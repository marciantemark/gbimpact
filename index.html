We Need More
Places to Learn.

Too many children in our area enter school without the preparation they need. Families need affordable early learning and more places for children to grow through the summer. We're preparing a campus that can help meet both needs.

Our plans include kindergarten readiness (ages 2–5), summer enrichment (rising K–8), and future lifelong-learning classes for the community.

Families from all backgrounds are welcome. You do not need to attend Greater Baltimore Church.

A Community Learning Center

Learning That
Welcomes Everyone.

Our programs will bring children and families together around four shared values:

  • Kindness: Caring for others.
  • Curiosity: Finding joy in learning.
  • Responsibility: Learning to make good choices.
  • Community: Respecting and learning from one another.

Founded by Greater Baltimore Church of Christ. Open to everyone. No church membership or attendance is required. Our programs welcome children and families of every faith and background and do not include religious instruction.
